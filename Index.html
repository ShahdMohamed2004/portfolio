Visual Timeline Grammar Framework

Research paper PDF

A quasi-experimental study testing whether drawing tenses on a timeline improves how EFL students grasp Past Simple, Continuous, and Perfect forms, run with ENG90 students from the Faculty of Dentistry and supervised by Dr. Shereen Effat.

Engagement rose 87%, a statistically significant gain (p = 0.002).
